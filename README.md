### About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the **University of Central Florida** ([Computational Biology Lab](https://www.cs.ucf.edu/compbio/people/)), working at the intersection of computational research and software engineering.

My work spans **single-cell multi-omics & computational biology**, **quantitative finance**, **AI privacy & security**, and **high-performance software systems**:
- **Single-Cell Multi-Omics & Computational Biology:** Developing delay-aware, enhancer-gated Neural ODEs for gene regulatory network inference (**ChronoGRN**, **Causal Dynamic GRN**), single-cell foundation model embedding transfer, and multi-omics cancer patient stratification.
- **Quantitative Finance & Market Microstructure:** Building low-latency orderbook/arbitrage simulators (**Orderbook**), analytical Black–Scholes options engines (**TradeSim**), event prediction markets (**Tejas Trading**), and the **Rust → Quant** interactive curriculum.
- **AI Security, Privacy & Architecture:** Auditing synthetic data leakage (**SyntheticShield**), safeguarding RAG/LLM pipelines (**RAGGuard**, **PromptLeakDetector**, **ModelCardSec**), and designing QoS-aware CPU–AI DRAM schedulers (**FairDRAM**).
