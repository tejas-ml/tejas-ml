### About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the **University of Central Florida** ([Computational Biology Lab](https://www.cs.ucf.edu/compbio/people/)), working at the intersection of computational research and software engineering.

My work spans **clinical AI**, **AI privacy and security**, **quantitative finance**, **computational biology**, and **high-performance systems**:
- **Clinical AI:** Building a retrieve-then-adjudicate engine that parses unstructured notes and ranks recruiting studies against inclusion and exclusion criteria (**[HelixMatch](https://github.com/tejas-ml/trial-matchmaking)**), plus RPM deterioration modeling (**AURA Clinical**) and healthcare claims-fraud review (**Aegis**).
- **AI Security and Privacy:** Auditing memorization and membership leakage in synthetic data generators (**[SyntheticShield](https://github.com/tejas-ml/SyntheticShield)**) and inserting an interpretable privacy firewall between retrieval and generation (**[RAGGuard](https://github.com/tejas-ml/RAGGuard)**), with related work on prompt-leak detection and ML security risk cards (**PromptLeakDetector**, **ModelCardSec**).
- **Quantitative Finance and Market Microstructure:** A Kalshi-style paper prediction market with a complementary YES/NO matching engine (**[Tejas Trading](https://github.com/tejas-ml/tejas-trading)**), a Black–Scholes options desk (**[TradeSim](https://github.com/tejas-ml/TradeSim)**), a limit-order-book and arbitrage simulator (**[Orderbook](https://github.com/tejas-ml/orderbook)**), and the **Rust → Quant** interactive curriculum.
- **Systems and Computational Biology:** QoS-aware CPU–AI DRAM scheduling (**[FairDRAM](https://github.com/tejas-ml/tejas-ml/tree/main/projects/FairDRAM)**), cross-cohort single-cell embedding transfer, and multi-omics cancer patient stratification.
